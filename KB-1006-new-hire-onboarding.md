# BOYLES IT SOLUTIONS | INTERNAL KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 📄 KB-1006: Day 1 Workspace Set-Up Checklist
**Category:** Employee Onboarding & Identity  
**Target Audience:** New Hires and Onboarding Managers  
**Last Updated:** May 2026  

---

### Welcome to Boyles IT Solutions! 👋
Your digital workspace has been fully provisioned by our systems team. To ensure a smooth transition and verify that your hardware, identity configurations, and application access layers are aligned, please complete this step-by-step checklist on your first morning.

---

### 🟢 Checklist Phase 1: Hardware & Local Network Connectivity
Before jumping into your software apps, let's verify your physical workspace setup is operational.

- [ ] **Monitor & Peripheral Check:** Verify your laptop is securely connected to your primary workstation monitor and power supply. 
- [ ] **Secure Local Network Connection:** Connect to the corporate network via your designated hardware routing link. 
  * *Remote Users:* Confirm your home Wi-Fi or physical Ethernet cable link is active before launching your system parameters.

---

### 🔵 Checklist Phase 2: Core Identity & Dashboard Access
Now, let's authenticate into the central control planes of your day-to-day workflow.

- [ ] **Access the Central IT Hub:** Open your secure browser (e.g., Brave) and navigate to the corporate intranet dashboard.
- [ ] **Verify Core Applications:** Ensure your user profile successfully populates the following application links on your dashboard layout:
  * Email Client Portal
  * Shared Team File Directories
  * Internal Project Boards
- [ ] **Execute Initial Session Sync:** Navigate to `http://portal.boyles-it.local/sync` to establish your active workspace directory token. *(Refer to **KB-1004** if your session encounters a loading loop).*

---

### 🟡 Checklist Phase 3: Hardware Inventory Sign-Off
At Boyles IT Solutions, we maintain strict control over our physical infrastructure assets to ensure security compliance.

- [ ] **Verify Asset Tag Numbers:** Locate the physical barcode sticker on the back of your laptop and monitor.
- [ ] **Log Into Asset Manager:** Navigate to the **Boyles IT SnipeIT Asset Management Portal**.
- [ ] **Accept Your Asset Assignment:** Under your profile, click "Accept Assignment" for your assigned hardware assets. This officially signs off on your deployment receipt and updates our active inventory database.

---

### 🚀 Final Step: Say Hello!
Once all boxes are checked, log into the **Boyles IT Service Desk** and submit a **Low Priority** ticket under the category **Onboarding -> Complete**. 

Simply write: *"Everything is working perfectly, ready for action!"* This allows our Service Coordinator to close out your onboarding file and officially welcome you to the active directory pool.
